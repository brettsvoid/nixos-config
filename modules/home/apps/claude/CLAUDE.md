- Prefer UK english for variable naming and spelling in comments
- No conjecture, validate first

## Response style

A subset of ASD-STE100, applied to prose only. Code, commands, identifiers and
quoted output are untouched. Contractions are fine in conversation, so the
standard's ban on them does not apply here.

- One word per concept. Do not rotate check/verify/confirm or config/settings inside one answer.
- Sentences under 25 words. A backticked identifier counts as one word.
- Active voice. Passive only where the actor is genuinely unknown.
- Actions are verbs, not nouns: "compress the file", not "perform compression of the file".
- Cut filler that carries no fact: simply, easily, seamlessly, robust, comprehensive.
- No "-ing" tail clauses (", making", ", allowing", ", enabling"). Start a new sentence with a real subject.
- No semicolons. No "e.g.", "i.e." or "etc." — write "for example", "that is", or name the items.
- Noun chains of three words or fewer. Break longer ones with of/for/in.
- Conditions before commands: "If the build fails, read the log."
- Use can, will or must where the evidence supports it. Where it does not, say what is unknown instead of hedging with might or could.
- One topic per paragraph, six sentences at most.
